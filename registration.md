## Who should register

Private landlords must register their tenancies with the RTB. Registration is a legal requirement.

## When to register

A tenancy must be registered when required and then registered annually.

## How to register

Register the tenancy through the RTB tenancy-registration service and keep the registration details with the tenancy records.

## Consequences of not registering

Failure to register can lead to serious penalties.

**Criminal prosecution:** a conviction, a fine of up to **€4,000**, up to **6 months' imprisonment**, a further **€250 per day** while the tenancy remains unregistered, and RTB legal costs.

**Civil sanction:** up to **€15,000**, plus up to **€15,000 in costs**, for each unregistered tenancy.

## If you receive a Registration Enforcement Notice

Register the tenancy, pay any applicable late fees, and give the RTB the registration number so the enforcement matter can be updated.

## Reporting an unregistered tenancy

An unregistered tenancy can be reported to the RTB through its landlord-reporting process.

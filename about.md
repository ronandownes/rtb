## Purpose

This site is a practical working guide to residential tenancy rules, RTB processes, evidence and data.

## Approach

The aim is to explain the material clearly, distinguish current rules from historical rules, and make it quick to find the information needed for a particular issue.

## Scope

The site is an interpretation and information resource. It is not the RTB website and is not legal advice.

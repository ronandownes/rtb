Registration material will be kept concise: who must register, when registration and annual registration are required, what records matter, and what evidence can establish registration status.

This section will contain rent-setting rules, rent reviews, notices, RPZ rules, market-rent evidence and rent records. Historical rules will be dated rather than silently applying today’s rules to older events.

Dispute material will focus on the routes a user actually needs: submitting a dispute, adjudication, mediation, tribunal, evidence, remedies, determination orders and urgent or interim issues where applicable.

This will hold the official RTB termination-notice form/template, a requirement-by-requirement checklist and a comparison tool for examining a notice against the applicable statutory requirements.
